## 4. Patient & Support FAQ Runbook

### Q1: How does registration and account approval work?
**Answer:** Doctors sign up with their specialty, dialysis unit, and medical syndicate ID photo. Patients sign up with their contact info and nephrology profile. All self-registrations undergo clinical audit by the Super Administrator before activation. Users can tap the direct WhatsApp link (`+201080641670`) to expedite approval with the Senior Resident. Alternatively, doctors can register patients directly in the clinic with instant auto-generated credentials!

### Q2: How do doctor-generated patient passwords work?
**Answer:** When a doctor registers a patient in-clinic, the system auto-generates a password using the patient's phone number followed by 3 random letters without overlap (e.g. `01012345678Xkz`), ensuring no confusing characters (`0, O, 1, l, I`) are used so elderly patients can read it effortlessly from their printed clinical access card.

### Q3: How often do I need to complete the Adherence Questionnaire?
**Answer:** The questionnaire is scheduled once per month. You will complete your baseline survey upon first using the app. The app will then remind you after Month 1 (30 days), Month 2 (60 days), and Month 3 (90 days). Your doctor can also trigger an unscheduled survey if they need an updated clinical evaluation.

### Q4: How does DialyCare calculate my daily fluid limit?
**Answer:** The limit is calculated directly from your target dry weight set by your doctor:
- **Warning Limit:** $\text{Dry Weight} \times 20\text{ mL}$
- **Critical Emergency Limit:** $\text{Dry Weight} \times 26\text{ mL}$  
Exceeding the critical limit triggers an instant acoustic alarm warning you of acute pulmonary edema risk.

### Q5: Does DialyCare work without an internet connection?
**Answer:** Yes! DialyCare is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). All session logs, fluid entries, and survey responses are saved locally and synced automatically once you regain connectivity.

## 5. Medical Disclaimer & Privacy Compliance

### 5.1 Medical Advisory Statement
> **⚠️ IMPORTANT MEDICAL NOTICE:**  
> DialyCare is a digital tracking and educational companion designed to support chronic hemodialysis management. It is not an automated medical diagnostic device, nor does it replace professional evaluation, diagnosis, or prescription by a qualified nephrologist. In case of acute shortness of breath, sudden chest pain, extreme blood pressure spikes, or dialysis access bleeding, seek emergency medical care immediately.

### 5.2 Patient Privacy & Data Protection Guarantee
- **Local-First Data Ownership:** All health metrics, session weights, lab values, and survey responses reside securely in your browser's encrypted sandbox.
- **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** DialyCare does not contain third-party ad networks, trackers, or commercial telemetry scripts.
- **HIPAA & GDPR Alignment:** Multi-tenant access enforces strict patient ID boundaries; unauthorized cross-patient data access is technically prohibited.
