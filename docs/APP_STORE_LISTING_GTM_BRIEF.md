### 2.2 English Description
```markdown
DialyCare is the premier clinical companion and adherence platform engineered specifically for chronic hemodialysis patients, their families, and nephrology teams.

DialyCare bridges the critical interval between hemodialysis sessions, transforming complex renal guidelines into actionable daily guidance with instant cross-device cloud synchronization.

Key Features:
- ☁️ Instant Cross-Device Cloud Sync: Patients register seamlessly on smartphones; registrations appear live on clinic admin dashboards for clinical verification and 1-click enrollment.
- 💧 Precision Fluid Restriction & Dry Weight Safety: Automated dynamic fluid targets based on dry weight with Web Audio warning alarms preventing pulmonary edema.
- 📋 Monthly Treatment Adherence Survey: 20 validated Likert-scale questions assessing attendance, medications, renal diet, and fluid management.
- 🩺 14-Parameter Clinical Session Log: Comprehensive intradialytic record (pre/post weights, 5-point vitals, ultrafiltration volume, vascular access health).
- 💊 Renal Medication Regimen: Specific schedules for phosphate binders with meals, antihypertensives, and vitamin D analogs.
- 🧪 12-Parameter Lab Trends: Instant color-coded auditing of Serum Potassium, Phosphorus, Calcium, Hemoglobin, and Kt/V dialysis adequacy.
- 🛡️ Permanent Session Resilience: Zero-eviction session persistence across page reloads and network toggles.
- 🚨 Nephrology Viral & Emergency Profiles: Built-in HCV/HBV/HIV isolation protocol markers and emergency contact dialer.
```
